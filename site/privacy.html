Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

mdwisp is a Markdown viewer and editor for macOS, Linux and Windows, published by Gailleur Labs. This policy covers the app and this website.

What the app collects

Nothing. mdwisp has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no update check and no telemetry of any kind. It never makes a network request on its own. Your documents stay on your disk and are read only when you open them.

What leaves your computer

Only what you ask for. If a document embeds an image from the web and you turn on View ▸ Toggle Remote Images, the app fetches that image from the address in your document, and the site hosting it sees that request. Links open in your browser. Printing and PDF export use your system's own print service. None of this involves Gailleur Labs.

What the app stores

A few preferences on your own machine: window size and position, theme, zoom, whether the outline is shown, and whether you answered the one-time question about making mdwisp the default Markdown app. The Mac App Store version also keeps a record of folders you have granted access to, so images beside your documents keep loading. These stay in your user account and are never transmitted.

This website

mdwisp.com is a static page served by Vercel. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. Vercel, as the hosting provider, keeps standard server logs for operating the service; see Vercel's privacy policy. Downloads are served by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.

Open source

Every claim above can be checked. The complete source code is public at github.com/gailleur/mdwisp under the Apache License 2.0.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the repository, or write to Gailleur Labs through gailleur.com.
